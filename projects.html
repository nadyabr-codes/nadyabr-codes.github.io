Cute Animal Tile Puzzle

Android & iOS game

AndroidGoogle Play ↗ QR
iPhone / iPadApp Store ↗ QR

A cozy picture puzzle game I built with Kotlin Multiplatform and shipped on Android and iOS. Swap any two tiles to piece together 20 cute animal pictures, with 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5 boards to choose from. There’s no timer — just a quiet puzzle break, or a challenge to solve your favorite picture in fewer moves.
