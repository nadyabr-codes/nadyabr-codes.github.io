A little about me

I build Android apps that hold up over time.

I’m Nadya, a senior Android engineer with more than ten years of experience building consumer products — from new features to mature, large-scale apps.

My day-to-day work is mostly Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. I enjoy the practical side of engineering: clear architecture, dependable playback and billing flows, responsive interfaces, and code that the next person can comfortably work with.

I’ve worked across mobile, Android TV, and large-screen products, often alongside design, product, and backend teams. I also keep returning to my own apps and small experiments — trying out ideas, improving them, and learning from what changes along the way.

This site is where I collect those projects, notes from the codebase, and the occasional rabbit hole.
